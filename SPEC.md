# Iowa Local Business Directory — Build Specification (v3)

## 1. What We're Building

An SEO-optimized website listing every home service business in Iowa, starting with SE Iowa + Iowa City. Each business gets its own page with unique AI-written content. Hermes builds and maintains everything automatically.

**URL:** `directory.grasshopperlocal.com`

**Cost to run:** $0/month. No server. No database. Static site on GitHub Pages + Cloudflare Workers free tier.

## 4. Architecture (Resolved)

```
GitHub Pages (free static CDN)
├── directory.grasshopperlocal.com/
│   ├── index.html
│   ├── [city]/[category]/[business].html
│   ├── [city]/[category]/index.html
│   ├── guides/[slug].html              (traffic-driving articles — new)
│   ├── css/  js/  sitemap.xml  404.html

Cloudflare Worker (free tier, 100K req/day)
├── handle-lead.js          — receives lead form POSTs
├── handle-claim.js         — business claims listing
├── lead-router.js          — checks KV, routes to business
├── stripe-checkout.js      — dynamically creates Stripe Checkout Session
├── stripe-webhook.js       — confirms payment succeeded
└── kv-data/                — Workers KV: lead state, subscription state
```

**Key decisions made (audit conditions resolved):**
- ✅ **Email via SendGrid** free tier (100 emails/day, covers Phase 1)
- ✅ **Stripe: dynamic Checkout Sessions** via Worker (STRIPE_SECRET_KEY stored as env var — server-side, safe)
- ✅ **Lead hold mechanism defined**: 24-hour hold, email top 3 businesses with unique payment links, first to pay wins, then expire
- ✅ **Search: Lunr.js** for Phase 1 (free, no ads, works on static sites)
- ✅ **Traffic plan added**: Guide articles alongside directory pages

## 5. The Traffic Plan (What Gets Us Found)

The directory pages alone won't rank for months. So alongside the business listings, we publish guide articles that rank faster because they answer real questions people search for.

### Guide Articles (Generated by Hermes, Same Pipeline)

Each gets its own page under `directory.grasshopperlocal.com/guides/`:

| Article Type | Example | Why It Ranks |
|---|---|---|
| **City category guide** | "How to Choose a Plumber in Burlington, IA" | Google loves local guides. Low competition. |
| **Emergency guide** | "What to Do When Your Basement Floods in Iowa City" | High urgency = high CTR. People search this. |
| **Seasonal guide** | "Is Your Furnace Ready for Iowa Winter? A Checklist" | Seasonal search spikes. Evergreen content. |
| **Cost guide** | "How Much Does a Roofer Cost in Iowa? (2026 Pricing)" | Pricing queries = high intent. Well-ranked by Google. |

**Phase 1 content batch:** 20 guide articles (2-3 per category) generated and published alongside the business listings. Same cron, same pipeline. Hermes writes them with Claude, publishes to static HTML.

**Why this works:** Guide articles rank in Google's regular search results (not just local pack). They bring informational traffic that converts to lead form submissions. A person reading "How to Choose an HVAC Company in Iowa City" is 3x more likely to submit a lead than someone landing on a bare business listing page.

## 6. Monetization (Lead-First Model)

### Stream 1: Lead Sales (Day 1 — Only Pay for Results)

**Process:**

1. Homeowner lands on any page. Sees: **"Need a plumber? Get matched with a vetted local pro — free."**
2. Fills out: name, phone, city, category, job description
3. Cloudflare Worker receives the lead. Checks KV: does any business in this city+category subscribe to leads?
4. **If subscriber exists:** Worker creates a Stripe Checkout Session link ($25) and texts it to the subscriber. Business pays → lead SMS'd to them. Total time: ~2 minutes.
5. **If no subscriber:** Worker stores lead in KV with 24-hour TTL. Emails top 3 businesses in that category via SendGrid: "Someone in Burlington needs a plumber. First to pay $25 gets this exclusive lead. [Pay Now]"
6. First business to complete Stripe Checkout gets the lead texted to them. Others get: "This lead has been claimed."
7. If no one buys in 24 hours, lead expires. Homeowner sees: "Sorry, we couldn't match you. Try searching our directory directly."

**Pricing:**

| Category | Our Price | Angi/HomeAdvisor | Savings |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plumber | $25 | $25-60 (shared) | Cheaper + exclusive |
| HVAC | $25 | $25-75 (shared) | Cheaper + exclusive |
| Electrician | $20 | $30-70 (shared) | Cheaper + exclusive |
| Roofer | $30 | $75-100 (shared) | 60-70% cheaper |
| Locksmith | $20 | $20-40 (shared) | Same price, exclusive |
| Landscaper | $15 | $15-40 (shared) | Cheaper + exclusive |
| Pest Control | $15 | $20-50 (shared) | Cheaper + exclusive |

### Stream 2: Display Ads (AdSense or Media.net)

Apply after site has content and some traffic. If rejected, use Media.net (easier approval for directory sites). Not relied on for first 6 months.

### Stream 3: Premium Listings ($29/mo)

Not offered until directory has proven traffic (500+ unique visitors/month). When traffic exists, businesses who see their listing getting views will pay to stand out. Until then, every listing is free.
